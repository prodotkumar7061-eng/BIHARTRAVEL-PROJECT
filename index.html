NALANDA

Ancient Seat of Learning

Nalanda University duniya ki sabse purani aur prasiddh universities mein se ek hai, jo Bihar mein sthit hai. Iski sthapna 5th Century (Gupta Empire) ke dauran hui thi.

Yahan poori duniya (jaise China, Korea, Japan, aur Tibet) se hazaron students aur scholars padhne aate the. Prasiddh Chinese yatri Xuanzang ne bhi yahan rehkar padhai ki thi.

Is jagah ke khandar (ruins) aaj bhi iske mahan itihaas ki gawaahi dete hain, jise UNESCO World Heritage site ka darja mila hua hai.

BODHGAYA

The Land of Enlightenment

Bodhgaya duniya bhar ke Buddhist dharm ke logon ke liye sabse pavitra sthal hai. Ye vahi jagah hai jahan Mahabodhi vriksh (Tree) ke niche Rajkumar Siddhartha ko gyan (Enlightenment) prapt hua tha aur ve "Gautam Buddha" kehlaye.

Yahan ka mukhya aakarshan **Mahabodhi Temple** hai, jise UNESCO ne World Heritage Site ghoshit kiya hai. Iske sath hi yahan 80-feet unchi Great Buddha Statue aur alag-alag deshon (jaise Japan, Thailand, aur Tibet) ke sundar monasteries bane hue hain.

Har saal yahan lakhon deshi-videshi paryatak shanti aur dhyan (meditation) ki khoj mein aate hain. Yahan ka vatavaran behad shant aur adhyatmik hai.

RAJGIR

The Modern Wonder: Glass Skywalk

Rajgir Bihar ka ek behad hi khoobsurat aur aitihasik shehar hai, jo charo taraf se panch pahadiyon se ghira hua hai. Aaj ke samay mein yahan ka sabse bada aakarshan **Rajgir Glass Bridge (Skywalk)** hai, jo India ke sabse pehle aur chuninda glass bridges mein se ek hai.

Yeh glass bridge ghaney jangalon aur pahadiyon ke beech bani **Nature Safari** mein sthit hai. Is par chalte waqt niche gahri ghati dikhai deti hai, jo paryatakon ko ek behad romanchak (thrilling) anubhav deti hai.

Glass Bridge ke alawa Rajgir mein **Vishwa Shanti Stupa**, exciting Ropeway, **Garam Kund** (Hot Springs), aur Venu Van jaise kai behad lokpriya ghumne layak sthal hain, jahan salo saal logon ki bheed rehti hai.
